How it works

Three steps to a covered-call income plan

No options PhD required. Screen, evaluate, and place trades on names you already trust.

1

Filter for quality

Start from a universe of liquid, fundamentally sound stocks — screen by sector, dividend, beta, and balance-sheet health.

2

Rank the call options

We score every viable strike on static yield, downside cushion, and if-called return — so the best risk/reward floats to the top.

3

Pocket the premium

Write the call, collect cash up front, and track your positions. Roll or let it get called — either way you keep the credit.
